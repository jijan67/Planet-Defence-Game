Features include score tracking, high score system, visual effects, and responsive design for all devices.

## 🌌 Overview
Planet Defence is an action-packed space shooter where you defend your planet from oncoming asteroids. Rotate your ship to aim at incoming threats and destroy them before they reach your planet.

## 🎮 Game Features

- **Fast-paced gameplay:** Defend against increasingly difficult waves of asteroids
- **Intuitive controls:** Aim with your mouse and click to shoot
- **Progressive difficulty:** Game speed increases the longer you survive
- **Visual feedback:** Explosions, particle effects, and screen shake for an immersive experience
- **Sound effects:** Dynamic audio for shooting, explosions, and game events
- **High score system:** Beat your best score and see a special celebration
- **Responsive design:** Play on any device with appropriate UI scaling

## 🔧 Technologies Used

- **HTML5 Canvas**
- **Vanilla JavaScript**
- **CSS3 animations**
- **Local Storage for saving high scores**

## 🚀 How to Play

- Click "START GAME" on the initial screen
- Move your mouse to rotate your spaceship
- Click to shoot lasers at incoming asteroids
- Destroy asteroids before they hit your planet
- Beat your high score!

## 🛠️ Installation
